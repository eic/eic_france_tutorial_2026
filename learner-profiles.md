---
title: Learner Profile
---

This lesson targets attendees of the [EIC France 2026 Workshop](https://indico.in2p3.fr/event/40260/overview). This includes students, postdocs and researchers with a range of ePIC/EIC experience. It assumes basic familiarity with the shell and ROOT, but no familiarity with ePIC software. However, even for those with experience, it should offer some new advice, tips and examples that are new to even experienced users.
